Our AI reads five sensors on each machine and from those five numbers alone it can tell you whether a failure is coming, what type it is, how many days are left, whether the machine is behaving abnormally right now, and when to schedule maintenance. With enough history it also tracks how health has evolved over time, filters out sensor noise, and blends every signal into one unified score — 0 to 100. The higher it is, the healthier the machine. The lower it drops, the faster you need to act.
