\chapter*{General Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{General Introduction}

Industrial maintenance is a central concern for any production company: an unanticipated failure on a manufacturing line leads to costly downtime, quality loss, and risks to operator safety. Traditional asset management approaches (EAM \textemdash{} \emph{Enterprise Asset Management}), based on corrective maintenance or preventive maintenance planned at fixed intervals, have reached their limits: they do not leverage machine operating data to anticipate actual failures.

It is within this context that the end-of-studies project carried out at SagemCom takes place: the design and implementation of an EAM platform integrating predictive maintenance capabilities through Machine Learning, intelligent field support for technicians, and a document-based conversational assistant.

The problem addressed is the following: \emph{how can a single platform unify traditional industrial asset management (users, machines, work orders and intervention orders) with failure prediction capabilities and intelligent support for maintenance teams?}

This report is structured into five chapters. The first chapter presents the general context of the internship, the host company, and the problem statement. The second chapter covers requirements analysis and specification. The third chapter details the system design. The fourth chapter describes the implementation carried out. The fifth chapter presents the tests performed and the results obtained. The report closes with a general conclusion that addresses the problem statement and outlines avenues for improvement.
