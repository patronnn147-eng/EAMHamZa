09 — Requirements · Functional

What the system must do.

  • Manage machines, work orders & interventions
  • Predict failure, type, RUL & anomalies (P1–P6)
  • Auto-draft procurement & maintenance schedules
  • Role-based dashboards & alert routing
  • RAG document Q&A grounded in live ML state
  • Readiness scoring, KPIs & maintenance timeline
09 — Requirements · Non-Functional

How well it must do it.

Performance

Parallel RAG + ML fetch; graceful rule-based fallback when the ML service is down.

Security

JWT auth, server-side role guards, S3 object isolation, secrets out of code.

Reliability

Non-fatal alerts, deduped drafts, 99 passing tests in CI.

Scalability

Containerized microservices, independently deployable.

Usability

Business language only — never "P4" or "Weibull" to users.

Maintainability

Two-pipeline ML design, versioned models, linted code.

12 — The AI Engine

Seven models. Two pipelines. One verdict.

Pipeline A · P1–P6

  • P1 Failure probability · P2 Failure type
  • P3 RUL (XGBoost) · P4 Behavioral anomaly
  • P5 Priority · P6 Maintenance schedule

Pipeline B · Wave 2 — DST Fusion

  • CUSUM · Kalman · Cox survival
  • Mahalanobis health · PINN
  • Dempster-Shafer → unified health score
